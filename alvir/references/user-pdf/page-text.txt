PAGE 7
Why this is not yet a true 3D mesh
• Instagram posts are compressed, single-viewpoint videos and photos
• No overlapping drone grid: photogrammetry needs 70–80% overlap between shots
• No camera position, GPS, or altitude data in the posts
• Thumbnails are about 1080 px — too low for fine detail
• So this deck is a visual reconstruction brief, not a measured 3D model
Honest limit — do not present the images as a surveyed model


PAGE 8
How to get a real 3D model
• Fly a drone grid over the village: nadir (straight down) plus oblique shots, 70–80% overlap
• Cover the rooftops, the rocky mound, Afshan Park, and the ancient tree
• Add ground control points with GPS, and note the 1,798 m elevation
• Process in photogrammetry software (e.g. OpenDroneMap, Metashape, or RealityCapture)
• Use the Instagram aerial video as the flight-planning reference, not the source data
Next step: a drone survey — the Instagram images alone cannot build the mesh
