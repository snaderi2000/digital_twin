APPLIED THEME
The separate Blender material study uses warmer mottled clay, localized fine
fissures, subdued green gates, weathered wood, muted metal roofs and foliage.
It preserves geometry, building material assignments and the old/new mixture.
Images are not pasted onto arbitrary facades. See data/visual-theme.json in the
project and output/alvir_reference_theme.blend. This is an appearance study,
not a completed photographic digital twin or a landmark reconstruction.
